# M5 Machine Learning — course plan

## Official basis

MSc Computer Science M5 Machine Learning, MQF Level 7, 10 ECTS. The approved descriptor governs LO1–LO8, topic coverage, total workload and assessment. The original institutional PDF is retained in `docs/Machine Learning.pdf` as existing reference material; check publication permissions before redistribution.

- Teaching sessions: **16 × 3 hours = 48 hours**.
- The university separately allocates **2 certification-exam hours**, distinct from the module's 3-hour written exam.
- Hybrid teaching: **9 synchronous online + 7 campus** sessions, subject to timetable confirmation.
- Assessment weights: **written examination 60%**, **Kaggle capstone 30%**, **presentation 10%**.
- Teaching platforms: Google Meet and Google Colab; Kaggle for capstone work.

## Expected material for each session

- `README.md` — goals, prerequisites, timetable, links to material and completion notes.
- `lecture.md` — detailed narrative teaching notes and examples.
- `slides.md` and preferably `slides.pptx` — slide source and editable presentation.
- `exercises.md` — guided practice and independent activities, without protected solutions.
- `student-lab.ipynb` (or `lab.ipynb`) — executable Colab practice when appropriate.
- Additional reference files only when they serve the lesson.

Already-created files such as S01's `brief.md`, `completion.md`, `references.md` and `manifest.json` may be retained; no packaging manifest is mandatory for future sessions.

## Publishing workflow

1. Choose Sxx and check approved outcomes against the existing curriculum.
2. Prepare or improve material using the optional course-builder skill.
3. Check that the examples work, the academic claims and references are sound, and the slides are readable.
4. Update `COURSE_STATUS.md` and commit/push to `master` when authorized.

**No Project Board, issue, branch, PR or continuous-integration approval is needed.** Professor approval of academic content remains a separate decision. Do not publish private assessment or student information.
